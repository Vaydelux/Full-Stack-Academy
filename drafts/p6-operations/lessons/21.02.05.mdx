## Applied engineering walkthrough

A request deadline is the total available time, whereas an attempt timeout bounds one call. Retries consume both time and dependency capacity. An ambiguous write needs stable idempotency identity or reconciliation; permanent errors should not retry.

### Diagnose the concrete failure

Evaluate retryDecision for transient and permanent failure, exhausted deadline and non-idempotent operation. Model elapsed time for multiple attempts and confirm the next attempt fits the remaining budget.

### Independent exercise and evidence

Design a single retry-owning layer with capped attempts, jitter and propagated deadlines. Record expected decision and actual elapsed time when a real local transport is available.
