# CAP tradeoffs and practical consistency

> Unpublished instructor manuscript. Local model tests are supplemental, not external runtime qualification.

## Learning outcomes

Explain the relevant engineering tradeoff, define a measurable experiment and recognize the boundary of the available evidence.

## Context

Two replicas cannot communicate during a write.

## Step 1 — Mental model

CAP describes consistency and availability during partitions, not a fixed pick-two menu under normal operation.

## Step 2 — Guided practice

Sketch a partition between replicas; choose reject versus accept conflicting writes for inventory and explain business impact.

Use synthetic data and a disposable authorized environment. Record the expected result before the experiment.

## Step 3 — Acceptance

**Positive:** Choice preserves the explicitly selected invariant under partition.

**Negative:** Claiming both linearizable writes and unconditional availability under partition fails.
