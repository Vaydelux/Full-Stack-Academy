# Bulkheads and backpressure

> Unpublished instructor manuscript. Local model tests are supplemental, not external runtime qualification.

## Learning outcomes

Explain the relevant engineering tradeoff, define a measurable experiment and recognize the boundary of the available evidence.

## Context

One slow endpoint occupies every worker.

## Step 1 — Mental model

Bulkheads isolate resource pools; backpressure bounds work rather than allowing unbounded queues.

## Step 2 — Guided practice

Construct a disposable pool with two workers and a maximum queue of three; fill it with synthetic tasks.

Use synthetic data and a disposable authorized environment. Record the expected result before the experiment.

## Step 3 — Acceptance

**Positive:** Other isolated work still runs; saturation rejects or delays explicitly.

**Negative:** Unbounded queue growth and starvation fail.
