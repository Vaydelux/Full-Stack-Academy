# Timeouts and retries

> Unpublished instructor manuscript. Local model tests are supplemental, not external runtime qualification.

## Learning outcomes

Explain the relevant engineering tradeoff, define a measurable experiment and recognize the boundary of the available evidence.

## Context

A chain of services retries at every hop.

## Step 1 — Mental model

Deadlines and retry budgets limit amplification; idempotency and transient-failure classification protect effects.

## Step 2 — Guided practice

Run retryDecision with remainingMs zero, maxAttempts reached and idempotent false; model one retry layer.

Use synthetic data and a disposable authorized environment. Record the expected result before the experiment.

## Step 3 — Acceptance

**Positive:** Retries stop at deadline, budget or unsafe operation.

**Negative:** Retrying permanent failures or ambiguous non-idempotent writes fails.

## Qualification boundary

Retry decision teaching model only; no network partition, broker, service discovery, replication or multi-region benchmark.

## Applied engineering walkthrough

Retries multiply across layers when each service independently retries a downstream failure. Propagate a total deadline and choose a bounded retry owner. A transient error is not enough to justify repeating an unsafe operation; unknown write outcomes need idempotency or reconciliation.

### Diagnose the concrete failure

Run retryDecision with a safe transient failure and then exhausted time, maximum attempts, permanent failure and non-idempotent input. Compare the resulting decisions. Sketch three layers each retrying and identify the potential multiplication of calls.

### Independent exercise and evidence

Design one retry policy with capped attempts, elapsed deadline and jitter. Record decisions and actual transport timings separately, including uncertain external write behavior.

**Evidence boundary:** These are practice instructions, not recorded results. Preserve the existing specification, model and unpublished status. Actual distributed runtime qualification remains independent.
