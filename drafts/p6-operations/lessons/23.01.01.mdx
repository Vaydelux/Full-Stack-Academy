# Cohesion coupling and SOLID

> Unpublished instructor manuscript. Local model tests are supplemental, not external runtime qualification.

## Learning outcomes

Explain the relevant engineering tradeoff, define a measurable experiment and recognize the boundary of the available evidence.

## Context

A service class handles orders, email, billing and formatting.

## Step 1 — Mental model

High cohesion groups related decisions; coupling measures dependency and change impact. SOLID guides design, not class count.

## Step 2 — Guided practice

Draw responsibilities and dependency edges; separate order rules from email delivery using a port.

Use synthetic data and a disposable authorized environment. Record the expected result before the experiment.

## Step 3 — Acceptance

**Positive:** Order policy can change without modifying the sender.

**Negative:** New cyclic dependencies or abstractions without a reason fail.
