## Applied engineering walkthrough

DNS records map names to service addresses, while resolver caching and TTL influence how long an earlier answer may persist. CDN caching of HTTP responses is a distinct layer. Changing DNS does not instantly move all clients, and cached assets can outlive an origin change.

### Diagnose the concrete failure

Draw two synthetic resolvers, one holding the previous record and another resolving the new one. Model a migration during the TTL window and verify both origins remain compatible. Plan independent CDN cache invalidation where the application requires it.

### Independent exercise and evidence

Design a domain cutover with lower TTL preparation, health verification, compatible origins and rollback conditions. Record actual DNS and HTTP observations only during an authorized test.
