# Signed media URLs

> Instructor draft. Local models do not qualify native platforms, real providers or production deployments.

## Learning outcomes

Explain the engineering decision, reproduce or design the positive and negative experiment, and identify the remaining evidence.

## Context

A private media URL is forwarded to another user.

## Step 1 — Mental model

Signed URLs are scoped bearer capabilities with short expiry; authorization must precede issuance and cache policy must respect it.

## Step 2 — Guided practice

Design synthetic object ID, expiry and audience policy; test expired and wrong-object capability in a real local signer.

Use synthetic data, a disposable environment and authorized accounts only. Record expected behavior before execution.

## Step 3 — Acceptance

**Positive:** Valid scoped capability retrieves intended object.

**Negative:** Expired or altered capability fails.

Inspect the complementary `mediaAllowed` teaching model. Extend its assertions when relevant; it is not an implementation of the full external technology.
