# Lab map-backed search

> Instructor draft. Local models do not qualify native platforms, real providers or production deployments.

## Learning outcomes

Explain the engineering decision, reproduce or design the positive and negative experiment, and identify the remaining evidence.

## Context

A learner implements nearby search.

## Step 1 — Mental model

A geospatial search combines validated points, scoped query, correct distance and bounded result count.

## Step 2 — Guided practice

Run validPoint; build disposable PostGIS search with known distance fixtures and map/list UI.

Use synthetic data, a disposable environment and authorized accounts only. Record expected behavior before execution.

## Step 3 — Acceptance

**Positive:** Returned authorized points meet radius and ordering contract.

**Negative:** Foreign locations, invalid points or unbounded results fail.

Inspect the complementary `validPoint` teaching model. Extend its assertions when relevant; it is not an implementation of the full external technology.

## Qualification boundary

Coordinate validation model only; real PostGIS geometry, index, map rendering and location privacy require separate acceptance.

## Knowledge check

1. What is the invariant and trust boundary?
2. Which negative result falsifies an unsafe design?
3. Which real tool or reviewer must supply missing evidence?

**Assessment:** Record exact fixture input, expected and actual output, negative evidence and not-run checks. Do not substitute a design or model for a real acceptance result.
