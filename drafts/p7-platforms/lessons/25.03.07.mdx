# Geo privacy

> Instructor draft. Local models do not qualify native platforms, real providers or production deployments.

## Learning outcomes

Explain the engineering decision, reproduce or design the positive and negative experiment, and identify the remaining evidence.

## Context

A map exposes exact home locations unnecessarily.

## Step 1 — Mental model

Location data can be sensitive; minimize precision, retention and audience, and obtain appropriate consent.

## Step 2 — Guided practice

Use synthetic coordinates; compare coarse display with restricted exact record and deny foreign tenant access.

Use synthetic data, a disposable environment and authorized accounts only. Record expected behavior before execution.

## Step 3 — Acceptance

**Positive:** Public view contains only approved precision.

**Negative:** Precise private location leaks or persistent tracking fails.

## Applied engineering walkthrough

Location information can reveal sensitive patterns even when names are removed. Collect only the precision and frequency required for the feature, restrict who can access exact records and define retention. Aggregation or rounding reduces some exposure but is not an anonymity guarantee.

### Diagnose the concrete failure

Use synthetic coordinates belonging to two tenants. Compare a coarse public display to a restricted exact record, and verify that a foreign tenant cannot obtain the precise value through search or export.

### Independent exercise and evidence

Create a data inventory covering collection, precision, audience, logs, backups and deletion. Test authorization and retention using synthetic records; document unresolved privacy and consent review.
