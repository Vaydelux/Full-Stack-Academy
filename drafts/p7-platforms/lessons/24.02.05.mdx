# Deleted records and tombstones

> Instructor draft. Local models do not qualify native platforms, real providers or production deployments.

## Learning outcomes

Explain the engineering decision, reproduce or design the positive and negative experiment, and identify the remaining evidence.

## Context

A deleted task is present on an offline device.

## Step 1 — Mental model

Tombstones preserve deletion history until replicas have safely passed the relevant watermark.

## Step 2 — Guided practice

Run the tombstone test, then attempt a stale update from an earlier version.

Use synthetic data, a disposable environment and authorized accounts only. Record expected behavior before execution.

## Step 3 — Acceptance

**Positive:** The stale update is refused and deletion remains.

**Negative:** Resurrecting a deleted task fails.
