# Model routing and fallbacks

> Instructor draft. Local models do not qualify native platforms, real providers or production deployments.

## Learning outcomes

Explain the engineering decision, reproduce or design the positive and negative experiment, and identify the remaining evidence.

## Context

A model endpoint times out.

## Step 1 — Mental model

Fallback routing must retain safety policy, output schema, deadline and budget across providers.

## Step 2 — Guided practice

Simulate timeout and one bounded fallback with mock models; verify citations and output schema.

Use synthetic data, a disposable environment and authorized accounts only. Record expected behavior before execution.

## Step 3 — Acceptance

**Positive:** Fallback preserves policy and records actual usage.

**Negative:** Retry loops, unsafe fallback or silent cost increase fails.
