# Retrieval and chunking

> Instructor draft. Local models do not qualify native platforms, real providers or production deployments.

## Learning outcomes

Explain the engineering decision, reproduce or design the positive and negative experiment, and identify the remaining evidence.

## Context

A large handbook must be retrieved in useful sections.

## Step 1 — Mental model

Chunking trades context coherence against search granularity; preserve source and offsets and measure retrieval recall.

## Step 2 — Guided practice

Split synthetic handbook by headings, compare chunks for fixed questions and track source IDs.

Use synthetic data, a disposable environment and authorized accounts only. Record expected behavior before execution.

## Step 3 — Acceptance

**Positive:** Relevant sections are retrieved with traceable source.

**Negative:** Chunks without provenance or missing relevant section fails.
