# Media cost modeling

> Instructor draft. Local models do not qualify native platforms, real providers or production deployments.

## Learning outcomes

Explain the engineering decision, reproduce or design the positive and negative experiment, and identify the remaining evidence.

## Context

A free-tier project must estimate video costs.

## Step 1 — Mental model

Estimate storage bytes, transfer, transcoding and concurrent streaming; validate current provider pricing separately.

## Step 2 — Guided practice

Use hypothetical media sizes and views, calculate scenario ranges and identify hard resource caps.

Use synthetic data, a disposable environment and authorized accounts only. Record expected behavior before execution.

## Step 3 — Acceptance

**Positive:** Costs include transfer and processing assumptions.

**Negative:** Ignoring egress or quoting assumed prices as current fails.

## Qualification boundary

Synthetic resource-bound model; no actual FFmpeg sandbox, CDN, HLS, WebRTC or moderation service acceptance.

## Applied engineering walkthrough

Media cost depends on input and rendition storage, delivered bytes, encoding work and simultaneous playback demand. Estimate using explicit workload assumptions, not a single average file size. Separate theoretical transfer volume from billable transfer under the chosen provider contract.

### Diagnose the concrete failure

Build a hypothetical workload with small and large assets, several rendition sizes and different view counts. Compare estimated storage and delivered volume, then vary watch completion and cache-hit assumptions.

### Independent exercise and evidence

Record low, base and high scenarios, resource quotas and an admission limit. Use hypothetical prices until current approved provider terms are verified; explain which variable most changes the estimate.

**Evidence boundary:** These are practice instructions, not recorded results. Preserve draft status and independently verify actual media tools, storage, playback, provider and reviewer evidence.
