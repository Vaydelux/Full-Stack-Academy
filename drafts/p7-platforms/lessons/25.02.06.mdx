# Evaluation datasets

> Instructor draft. Local models do not qualify native platforms, real providers or production deployments.

## Learning outcomes

Explain the engineering decision, reproduce or design the positive and negative experiment, and identify the remaining evidence.

## Context

An AI answer appears fluent but is unsupported.

## Step 1 — Mental model

Evaluation datasets contain expected facts, source relevance, refusal cases and adversarial inputs; avoid using only happy paths.

## Step 2 — Guided practice

Create labeled synthetic questions including unknown and foreign-tenant cases; score retrieval and answer separately.

Use synthetic data, a disposable environment and authorized accounts only. Record expected behavior before execution.

## Step 3 — Acceptance

**Positive:** Failures are visible and reproducible.

**Negative:** A citation string alone cannot count as factual correctness.

Inspect the complementary `citedAnswer` teaching model. Extend its assertions when relevant; it is not an implementation of the full external technology.

## Qualification boundary

Synthetic citation membership check; not evidence of factual grounding, model safety, embeddings or real provider behavior.

## Applied engineering walkthrough

Evaluate retrieval and generated answers separately. A relevant retrieved passage can still lead to an unsupported answer, while correct abstention may be the desired result for missing information. Include authorized, unauthorized, ambiguous and adversarial cases; keep evaluation fixtures separate from tuning data.

### Diagnose the concrete failure

Create labeled synthetic questions with expected facts, source identifiers and abstention behavior. Inject a plausible wrong answer and a foreign citation; score each dimension independently rather than collapsing everything into one accuracy number.

### Independent exercise and evidence

Write a regression rubric covering source recall, factual support, policy compliance, latency and usage. Compare two mock configurations and record individual failures, not just an aggregate score.

**Evidence boundary:** These are practice instructions, not recorded results. Preserve draft status; independently verify actual model, database, retrieval, provider and reviewer evidence.

## Knowledge check

1. What is the invariant and trust boundary?
2. Which negative result falsifies an unsafe design?
3. Which real tool or reviewer must supply missing evidence?

**Assessment:** Record exact fixture input, expected and actual output, negative evidence and not-run checks. Do not substitute a design or model for a real acceptance result.
