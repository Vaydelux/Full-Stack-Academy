# Event tracking and instrumentation

> Instructor draft. Local models do not qualify native platforms, real providers or production deployments.

## Learning outcomes

Explain the engineering decision, reproduce or design the positive and negative experiment, and identify the remaining evidence.

## Context

An analytics SDK retries a page-view event.

## Step 1 — Mental model

Event IDs, version, timestamp and consent scope are part of the instrumentation contract.

## Step 2 — Guided practice

Run aggregateEvents with duplicate view ID and distinct purchase ID; document schema and consent.

Use synthetic data, a disposable environment and authorized accounts only. Record expected behavior before execution.

## Step 3 — Acceptance

**Positive:** Exact duplicate is counted once in the model.

**Negative:** Duplicate retry inflating totals fails.

## Qualification boundary

In-memory count/deduplication model; no warehouse, materialized-view freshness, ClickHouse or dbt runtime qualification.

## Applied engineering walkthrough

An analytics event contract needs a stable event identity, schema version, occurrence time and scope for permitted use. Distinguish event time from receipt time, and avoid including unnecessary personal data. A replay with identical identity must not inflate an aggregate.

### Diagnose the concrete failure

Submit a synthetic view event twice and one purchase event with a distinct ID. Compare unique counts, then attempt conflicting content under the original ID and define explicit rejection or quarantine behavior.

### Independent exercise and evidence

Specify required fields, version compatibility and late-arrival handling. Record which events are eligible for analytics under the reviewed purpose and how withdrawn eligibility affects downstream records.
