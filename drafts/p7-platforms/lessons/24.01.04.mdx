# Android lifecycle

> Instructor draft. Local models do not qualify native platforms, real providers or production deployments.

## Learning outcomes

Explain the engineering decision, reproduce or design the positive and negative experiment, and identify the remaining evidence.

## Context

An Android app is backgrounded during an edit.

## Step 1 — Mental model

Lifecycle transitions may interrupt asynchronous work; persist intent before relying on in-memory state.

## Step 2 — Guided practice

In a disposable app edit a task, background and terminate it; relaunch and reconcile the queue.

Use synthetic data, a disposable environment and authorized accounts only. Record expected behavior before execution.

## Step 3 — Acceptance

**Positive:** The persisted edit survives and resumes once.

**Negative:** Lost or duplicate work after process death fails.
