# Bug Hunt lost sync

> Instructor draft. Local models do not qualify native platforms, real providers or production deployments.

## Learning outcomes

Explain the engineering decision, reproduce or design the positive and negative experiment, and identify the remaining evidence.

## Context

A task disappears after airplane mode and relaunch.

## Step 1 — Mental model

Queue durability requires distinguishing sent, acknowledged and locally applied work.

## Step 2 — Guided practice

Use resumeQueue to replay only unacknowledged IDs; reproduce network loss and restart in actual app.

Use synthetic data, a disposable environment and authorized accounts only. Record expected behavior before execution.

## Step 3 — Acceptance

**Positive:** Pending operation is retained until confirmed.

**Negative:** Dropping work merely because a send was attempted fails.

Inspect the complementary `resumeQueue` teaching model. Extend its assertions when relevant; it is not an implementation of the full external technology.

## Qualification boundary

Synthetic queue acknowledgement model only; physical touch, OS background behavior, push delivery and store acceptance require device tests.

## Applied engineering walkthrough

Distinguish work that was sent, work acknowledged by the server and acknowledgement durably applied locally. Sending is not completion. Use a stable operation identifier and persist acknowledgement before removing queue entries; uncertain work must remain recoverable.

### Diagnose the concrete failure

Prepare three operations in different states, interrupt after remote acceptance and restart. Check resumeQueue eligibility, replay the uncertain operation with its original ID and inspect final effects.

### Independent exercise and evidence

Test loss before send, after send and during acknowledgement persistence. Record pending counts, server results and actual device restart behavior. Keep retry limits and explicit unresolved states visible.
