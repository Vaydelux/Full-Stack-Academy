# Lab source-cited assistant

> Instructor draft. Local models do not qualify native platforms, real providers or production deployments.

## Learning outcomes

Explain the engineering decision, reproduce or design the positive and negative experiment, and identify the remaining evidence.

## Context

A learner builds a source-cited assistant.

## Step 1 — Mental model

Retrieval must be authorized before generation; sources must actually support claims and unknown answers should abstain.

## Step 2 — Guided practice

Run citedAnswer membership checks, then build disposable retrieval pipeline and manually compare answer claims with sources.

Use synthetic data, a disposable environment and authorized accounts only. Record expected behavior before execution.

## Step 3 — Acceptance

**Positive:** Allowed citations exist and real grounding is reviewed separately.

**Negative:** Cross-tenant or fabricated citations fail.

Inspect the complementary `citedAnswer` teaching model. Extend its assertions when relevant; it is not an implementation of the full external technology.

## Qualification boundary

Synthetic citation membership check; not evidence of factual grounding, model safety, embeddings or real provider behavior.

## Knowledge check

1. What is the invariant and trust boundary?
2. Which negative result falsifies an unsafe design?
3. Which real tool or reviewer must supply missing evidence?
