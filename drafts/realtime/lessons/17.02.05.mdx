## Context

A company announcement is distributed to thousands of members.

This scenario connects directly to a real full-stack engineering concern: security, consistency, user experience and the cost of retries must be considered together.

## Misconception

Large fan-out needs provider backpressure and transactional persistence, not a giant synchronous loop.

## Applied engineering walkthrough

Fan-out expands one event into recipient-specific work. Large audiences need bounded batches, stable pagination and durable progress. Membership and preferences can change during delivery, so define whether eligibility is evaluated at event time or send time.

### Diagnose the concrete failure

Create an announcement for two synthetic recipients with a shared event identity. Verify both receive one item and replay does not duplicate either. Simulate an interrupted batch and resume from recorded progress.

### Independent exercise and evidence

Design a bounded fan-out worker with backpressure and per-recipient identity. Record expected and actual recipient counts, partial failure and eligibility timing.
