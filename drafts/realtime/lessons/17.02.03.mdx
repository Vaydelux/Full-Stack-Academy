# Email and push delivery

> Unpublished instructor manuscript. This topic is not available in the public Academy reader and has not passed Node 24/production browser qualification.

## Learning outcomes

## Context

The app sends alerts by email and mobile push.

This scenario connects directly to a real full-stack engineering concern: security, consistency, user experience and the cost of retries must be considered together.

## Step 1 — Explain the model

Separate intent, provider attempt, acknowledgment and terminal failure; use stable message IDs and per-channel consent.

The product interprets enqueue success as delivered and retries unsafe notifications indefinitely.

**Adversarial exercise:** Describe the smallest reproducible request or event sequence that exposes this failure. Record what data must remain private, what state must remain durable, and what a reconnecting client is allowed to replay.

## Misconception

Push services and email APIs have delivery semantics the in-memory notification example does not simulate.

## Applied engineering walkthrough

An email or push provider may acknowledge acceptance without confirming final delivery. Retry transient failures within a budget and distinguish permanent invalid recipients or revoked device tokens. Ambiguous acknowledgement requires stable operation identity.

### Diagnose the concrete failure

Use a fake provider returning acceptance, a transient timeout and a permanent rejection. Verify each yields a distinct recorded state and the timeout retry retains the original identity. Do not send real messages.

### Independent exercise and evidence

Design provider status mapping with accepted, delivered, bounced and failed states where supported. Record attempt outcomes and what actual provider receipts would establish.
