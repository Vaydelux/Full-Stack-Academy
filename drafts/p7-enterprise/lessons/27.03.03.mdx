# Notification queue

> Instructor draft. Local models do not qualify external identity, billing, privacy compliance or an actual capstone release.

## Learning outcomes

Explain the engineering decision, reproduce or design the positive and negative experiment, and identify the remaining evidence.

## Context

A task assignment sends a notification.

## Step 1 — Mental model

Durable queue and outbox separate transaction from external delivery; retries need stable identifiers.

## Step 2 — Guided practice

Implement a disposable assignment/outbox transaction and fake sender with duplicate and failed delivery.

Use synthetic data, a disposable environment and authorized accounts only. Record expected behavior before execution.

## Step 3 — Acceptance

**Positive:** One logical notification is tracked with retry outcome.

**Negative:** Email sent for rolled-back assignment or duplicate effect fails.
