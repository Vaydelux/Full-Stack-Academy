# Observability and alerts

> Instructor draft. Local models do not qualify external identity, billing, privacy compliance or an actual capstone release.

## Learning outcomes

Explain the engineering decision, reproduce or design the positive and negative experiment, and identify the remaining evidence.

## Context

An API failure must be detectable.

## Step 1 — Mental model

Logs, metrics, traces and alerts should describe user impact without leaking data; ownership and runbooks matter.

## Step 2 — Guided practice

Instrument isolated API, trigger bounded failure and verify alert/runbook path.

Use synthetic data, a disposable environment and authorized accounts only. Record expected behavior before execution.

## Step 3 — Acceptance

**Positive:** Failure is correlated and actionable.

**Negative:** Secret logging or unowned noisy alerts fail.
