# Subscription state machines

> Instructor draft. Local models do not qualify external identity, billing, privacy compliance or an actual capstone release.

## Learning outcomes

Explain the engineering decision, reproduce or design the positive and negative experiment, and identify the remaining evidence.

## Context

A subscription transitions through trial, active and unpaid states.

## Step 1 — Mental model

Use explicit allowed transitions; provider status and application entitlement are related but distinct.

## Step 2 — Guided practice

Create a synthetic state transition table and test active, canceled and expired dates in entitlement.

Use synthetic data, a disposable environment and authorized accounts only. Record expected behavior before execution.

## Step 3 — Acceptance

**Positive:** Access matches the documented policy and cutoff.

**Negative:** Unknown status or expired entitlement cannot grant access.

Inspect the complementary `entitlement` teaching model. Extend its assertions when relevant; it is not an implementation of the full external technology.

## Qualification boundary

Synthetic entitlement policy; no payment provider authenticity, tax, invoicing, real charges or durable billing integration verified.

## Applied engineering walkthrough

A provider subscription state and an application's entitlement are related but separate. Define permitted transitions, effective time and product access policy explicitly. Cancellation may take effect immediately or at period end depending on the reviewed contract; unknown states must not silently grant privileges.

### Diagnose the concrete failure

Create a table for trial, active, canceled and expired subscriptions using synthetic times. Exercise the exact cutoff and an unrecognized status, then compare the access decision with the policy.

### Independent exercise and evidence

Record state, effective time and reason for each transition. Include late events and a policy change, and distinguish payment provider reconciliation from local entitlement evaluation.

**Evidence boundary:** These are practice instructions, not recorded results. Preserve draft status; independently verify real sandbox signatures, database durability, billing and reviewer evidence.
