# SCIM provisioning

> Instructor draft. Local models do not qualify external identity, billing, privacy compliance or an actual capstone release.

## Learning outcomes

Explain the engineering decision, reproduce or design the positive and negative experiment, and identify the remaining evidence.

## Context

An employee leaves the organization.

## Step 1 — Mental model

SCIM provisioning synchronizes identities and groups; disabling identity must also revoke applicable access, not merely hide it.

## Step 2 — Guided practice

Design a synthetic create/update/deactivate event sequence and test repeated delivery and deactivation.

Use synthetic data, a disposable environment and authorized accounts only. Record expected behavior before execution.

## Step 3 — Acceptance

**Positive:** Disabled user loses intended access; repeated provisioning is safe.

**Negative:** Deactivated employee retaining access fails.

Inspect the complementary `enterpriseAccess` teaching model. Extend its assertions when relevant; it is not an implementation of the full external technology.

## Qualification boundary

Synthetic policy check on already-validated claims only; no actual SAML assertion, SCIM, MFA, token or identity-provider verification.

## Applied engineering walkthrough

SCIM is a provisioning contract, not an authentication protocol. Map external identities to stable internal identities and keep repeated operations safe. Deactivation must prevent applicable access and may require revocation of sessions and tokens; hiding an account in the UI does not revoke it.

### Diagnose the concrete failure

Rehearse synthetic create, attribute update, group removal and deactivate events, including duplicate delivery. Verify the internal membership transitions and whether subsequent protected requests still succeed.

### Independent exercise and evidence

Design conflict handling for duplicate external IDs, out-of-order updates and partial failures. Record an auditable reconciliation path and actual session revocation evidence separately from provisioning model results.

**Evidence boundary:** These are practice instructions, not recorded results. Preserve draft status and independently verify actual IdP, application authorization, database and reviewer evidence.

## Knowledge check

1. What is the invariant and trust boundary?
2. Which negative result falsifies an unsafe design?
3. Which real tool or reviewer must supply missing evidence?

**Assessment:** Record exact fixture input, expected and actual output, negative evidence and not-run checks. Do not substitute a design or model for a real acceptance result.
