# MFA and step up auth

> Instructor draft. Local models do not qualify external identity, billing, privacy compliance or an actual capstone release.

## Learning outcomes

Explain the engineering decision, reproduce or design the positive and negative experiment, and identify the remaining evidence.

## Context

A user initiates a high-risk settings change.

## Step 1 — Mental model

Step-up authentication requires recent stronger evidence for sensitive actions; ordinary sign-in may be insufficient.

## Step 2 — Guided practice

Evaluate enterpriseAccess with required MFA and missing claim; design an actual challenge and freshness test.

Use synthetic data, a disposable environment and authorized accounts only. Record expected behavior before execution.

## Step 3 — Acceptance

**Positive:** Validated fresh second factor allows intended operation.

**Negative:** Missing, stale or spoofed factor evidence fails.

Inspect the complementary `enterpriseAccess` teaching model. Extend its assertions when relevant; it is not an implementation of the full external technology.

## Qualification boundary

Synthetic policy check on already-validated claims only; no actual SAML assertion, SCIM, MFA, token or identity-provider verification.

## Applied engineering walkthrough

Step-up authentication requires stronger or fresher authentication evidence for a particular sensitive action. A client checkbox or decoded claim without trusted verification is insufficient. Define the required assurance, freshness and action scope, then enforce them on the server before the effect occurs.

### Diagnose the concrete failure

Use enterpriseAccess for a missing MFA claim and an allowed case. Extend the real test design with stale authentication evidence, canceled challenge and a changed target action. Verify denial leaves protected state untouched.

### Independent exercise and evidence

Design a challenge flow with bounded retries, recovery and audited completion. Record trusted evidence and the exact action gate; do not interpret the local policy model as an actual identity-provider integration.

**Evidence boundary:** These are practice instructions, not recorded results. Preserve draft status and independently verify actual IdP, application authorization, database and reviewer evidence.

## Knowledge check

1. What is the invariant and trust boundary?
2. Which negative result falsifies an unsafe design?
3. Which real tool or reviewer must supply missing evidence?

**Assessment:** Record exact fixture input, expected and actual output, negative evidence and not-run checks. Do not substitute a design or model for a real acceptance result.
