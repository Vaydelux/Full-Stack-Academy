# Encryption key management

> Instructor draft. Local models do not qualify external identity, billing, privacy compliance or an actual capstone release.

## Learning outcomes

Explain the engineering decision, reproduce or design the positive and negative experiment, and identify the remaining evidence.

## Context

An encrypted database backup needs key rotation.

## Step 1 — Mental model

Key lifecycle includes generation, storage, access, versioning, rotation, recovery and destruction; encryption alone is insufficient.

## Step 2 — Guided practice

Design synthetic key versions and restore old ciphertext with approved key, then rotate new writes.

Use synthetic data, a disposable environment and authorized accounts only. Record expected behavior before execution.

## Step 3 — Acceptance

**Positive:** Authorized recovery works under documented versions.

**Negative:** Lost key, exposed key or untested rotation fails.
