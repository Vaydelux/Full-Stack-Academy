# Docker compose and envs

> Instructor draft. Local models do not qualify external identity, billing, privacy compliance or an actual capstone release.

## Learning outcomes

Explain the engineering decision, reproduce or design the positive and negative experiment, and identify the remaining evidence.

## Context

A local app needs a reproducible multi-service environment.

## Step 1 — Mental model

Compose declares service dependencies, private networking, volumes, env boundaries and health checks.

## Step 2 — Guided practice

Create a disposable stack with app and DB; use synthetic secrets and inspect port exposure and readiness.

Use synthetic data, a disposable environment and authorized accounts only. Record expected behavior before execution.

## Step 3 — Acceptance

**Positive:** Stack starts reproducibly and DB is private.

**Negative:** Public DB or credentials in committed env fails.
