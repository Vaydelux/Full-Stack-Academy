# Lab retention policy

> Instructor draft. Local models do not qualify external identity, billing, privacy compliance or an actual capstone release.

## Learning outcomes

Explain the engineering decision, reproduce or design the positive and negative experiment, and identify the remaining evidence.

## Context

A learner designs a retention job.

## Step 1 — Mental model

Expiry identifies candidates for review; holds, applicable legal duties and safe execution remain separate.

## Step 2 — Guided practice

Run retentionDecision at exact cutoff with and without legal hold; produce dry-run candidate list and approval process.

Use synthetic data, a disposable environment and authorized accounts only. Record expected behavior before execution.

## Step 3 — Acceptance

**Positive:** Eligible items are reviewable; held records retained.

**Negative:** Automatic irreversible deletion on a boolean alone fails.

Inspect the complementary `retentionDecision` teaching model. Extend its assertions when relevant; it is not an implementation of the full external technology.

## Qualification boundary

Synthetic retention review model only; not legal advice or proof of compliance. Verify current law and organization-specific obligations with qualified counsel.

## Knowledge check

1. What is the invariant and trust boundary?
2. Which negative result falsifies an unsafe design?
3. Which real tool or reviewer must supply missing evidence?

**Assessment:** Record exact fixture input, expected and actual output, negative evidence and not-run checks. Do not substitute a design or model for a real acceptance result.
