# GDPR concepts and Philippine Data Privacy Act overview

> Instructor draft. Local models do not qualify external identity, billing, privacy compliance or an actual capstone release.

## Learning outcomes

Explain the engineering decision, reproduce or design the positive and negative experiment, and identify the remaining evidence.

## Context

A service operates in the Philippines and serves EU users.

## Step 1 — Mental model

GDPR and the Philippine Data Privacy Act establish distinct privacy frameworks; applicability depends on facts and jurisdiction.

## Step 2 — Guided practice

Compare official texts and regulator guidance for a hypothetical service; document questions for counsel rather than assuming identical obligations.

Use synthetic data, a disposable environment and authorized accounts only. Record expected behavior before execution.

## Applied engineering walkthrough

Privacy frameworks must be evaluated against the facts of a service and the relevant jurisdictions. Similar terminology does not establish identical duties, exceptions or enforcement. Separate engineering controls from legal conclusions and use current official texts and qualified review when determining applicability.

### Diagnose the concrete failure

Construct a hypothetical service with users, processors and backups in different jurisdictions. List the factual questions needed before determining which framework applies; do not assume user nationality alone settles the issue.

### Independent exercise and evidence

Prepare a comparison worksheet using official regulator material for the hypothetical scenario. Record the source date, unresolved interpretation and reviewer questions; engineering tests cannot certify legal compliance.

**Evidence boundary:** These are practice instructions, not recorded results. Preserve draft status; actual controls, legal applicability, runtime and independent review remain separate gates.
