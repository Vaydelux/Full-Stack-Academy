# Consent and retention

> Instructor draft. Local models do not qualify external identity, billing, privacy compliance or an actual capstone release.

## Learning outcomes

Explain the engineering decision, reproduce or design the positive and negative experiment, and identify the remaining evidence.

## Context

A user withdraws optional marketing consent.

## Step 1 — Mental model

Record purpose, lawful basis or consent where applicable, withdrawal and a reviewed retention policy; do not conflate consent with every processing basis.

## Step 2 — Guided practice

Design a synthetic preference history and retention table; test withdrawal without deleting unrelated required records.

Use synthetic data, a disposable environment and authorized accounts only. Record expected behavior before execution.

## Step 3 — Acceptance

**Positive:** Optional processing stops according to policy.

**Negative:** Continued optional processing or blanket deletion without review fails.

Inspect the complementary `retentionDecision` teaching model. Extend its assertions when relevant; it is not an implementation of the full external technology.

## Applied engineering walkthrough

Purpose, processing justification and retention are distinct decisions. Consent is not the universal basis for all processing, and withdrawal of an optional preference does not automatically remove records required for unrelated valid purposes. Model consent changes as attributable events and enforce the reviewed policy in downstream processing.

### Diagnose the concrete failure

Create synthetic optional analytics preferences and a separate necessary account record. Withdraw the optional preference and verify that new optional processing stops without incorrectly deleting unrelated records.

### Independent exercise and evidence

Draft a purpose and retention matrix with policy owner, trigger and exceptions. Test preference history and a failed downstream update, recording how reconciliation prevents continued unauthorized optional use.

**Evidence boundary:** These are practice instructions, not recorded results. Preserve draft status; actual controls, legal applicability, runtime and independent review remain separate gates.
