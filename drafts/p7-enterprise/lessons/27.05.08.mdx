## Learning outcomes

Explain the engineering decision, reproduce or design the positive and negative experiment, and identify the remaining evidence.

## Context

A learner completes the technical assessment.

## Step 1 — Mental model

A final defense evaluates implementation evidence, tradeoffs, recovery, cost and documentation, not memorized tool lists.

## Step 2 — Guided practice

Run defenseAcceptance; assemble commit-bound portfolio, demo, negative tests, runbook and independent review rubric.

Use synthetic data, a disposable environment and authorized accounts only. Record expected behavior before execution.

## Step 3 — Acceptance

**Positive:** Reviewer can reproduce core flow and challenge assumptions.

**Negative:** Pending or unverified evidence remains incomplete.

Inspect the complementary `defenseAcceptance` teaching model. Extend its assertions when relevant; it is not an implementation of the full external technology.
