# Audit trail

> Instructor draft. Local models do not qualify external identity, billing, privacy compliance or an actual capstone release.

## Learning outcomes

Explain the engineering decision, reproduce or design the positive and negative experiment, and identify the remaining evidence.

## Context

An admin needs to explain a task change.

## Step 1 — Mental model

Audit trail records actor, action, target, outcome and time; sensitive content excluded.

## Step 2 — Guided practice

Create, edit and deny synthetic actions; inspect scoped audit output and replay correlation.

Use synthetic data, a disposable environment and authorized accounts only. Record expected behavior before execution.

## Step 3 — Acceptance

**Positive:** Relevant events are traceable without secrets.

**Negative:** Missing denied action or editable unaudited history fails.

Inspect the complementary `workspaceAcceptance` teaching model. Extend its assertions when relevant; it is not an implementation of the full external technology.

## Qualification boundary

Synthetic integrated-feature rubric; actual upload, mail, Redis, websocket, search and audit behavior require live isolated tests.

## Applied engineering walkthrough

Audit events should capture actor, action, target, outcome, time and correlation without copying secrets or unnecessary task content. A successful mutation and a rejected attempt are different outcomes; define how each is recorded and who may inspect it. Durable audit integrity needs more than a console log.

### Diagnose the concrete failure

Create, edit and deny synthetic task actions. Trace a correlation identifier through the request and audit record, then attempt a foreign-tenant audit read. Simulate a failed audit write and inspect the documented behavior.

### Independent exercise and evidence

Design scoped audit queries, retention and protected evidence export. Record event identity, affected object and outcome alongside actual transaction evidence; test duplicate event handling and redaction of sensitive inputs.

**Evidence boundary:** These are practice instructions, not recorded results. Preserve draft status and independently verify actual API, storage, database, queue, cache, browser and reviewer evidence.

## Knowledge check

1. What is the invariant and trust boundary?
2. Which negative result falsifies an unsafe design?
3. Which real tool or reviewer must supply missing evidence?

**Assessment:** Record exact fixture input, expected and actual output, negative evidence and not-run checks. Do not substitute a design or model for a real acceptance result.
