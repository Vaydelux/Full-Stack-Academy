# Usage metering

> Instructor draft. Local models do not qualify external identity, billing, privacy compliance or an actual capstone release.

## Learning outcomes

Explain the engineering decision, reproduce or design the positive and negative experiment, and identify the remaining evidence.

## Context

An API bills by consumed units.

## Step 1 — Mental model

Metering records immutable usage identity, unit, time, tenant and aggregation window; reconcile late or duplicate events.

## Step 2 — Guided practice

Create ten synthetic usage records including one duplicate; aggregate unique records and compare invoice line.

Use synthetic data, a disposable environment and authorized accounts only. Record expected behavior before execution.

## Step 3 — Acceptance

**Positive:** Billable units reconcile with source records.

**Negative:** Duplicate events or mixed tenant usage inflate the invoice.

## Applied engineering walkthrough

Metering requires stable usage-event identity, tenant, unit, occurrence time and aggregation window. Keep units consistent, handle duplicate and late events, and preserve enough lineage to reconcile a disputed invoice. Separate the raw usage record from the derived charge calculation.

### Diagnose the concrete failure

Create ten synthetic records with one duplicate and one foreign-tenant event. Aggregate only eligible unique units for the target window and compare to a manually calculated expected total.

### Independent exercise and evidence

Specify rounding, late arrivals and correction records. Test a conflicting duplicate and a record exactly at the window boundary. Record source IDs and aggregate units without exposing real billing information.
