# PostgreSQL schema and migrations

> Instructor draft. Local models do not qualify external identity, billing, privacy compliance or an actual capstone release.

## Learning outcomes

Explain the engineering decision, reproduce or design the positive and negative experiment, and identify the remaining evidence.

## Context

A task table must support many organizations.

## Step 1 — Mental model

Relational constraints and migrations enforce stable IDs, tenant relationships and invariants.

## Step 2 — Guided practice

Create disposable PostgreSQL migration for organizations, members and tasks; test foreign-key violation and rollback.

Use synthetic data, a disposable environment and authorized accounts only. Record expected behavior before execution.

## Step 3 — Acceptance

**Positive:** Valid rows persist; invalid relationships denied.

**Negative:** Cross-tenant relationship or unreviewed destructive migration fails.
