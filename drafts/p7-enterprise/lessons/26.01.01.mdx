# SAML SSO architecture

> Instructor draft. Local models do not qualify external identity, billing, privacy compliance or an actual capstone release.

## Learning outcomes

Explain the engineering decision, reproduce or design the positive and negative experiment, and identify the remaining evidence.

## Context

A company wants employees to sign in through its identity provider.

## Step 1 — Mental model

SAML SSO exchanges signed assertions; validate issuer, audience, recipient, time and replay protection using a trusted library and metadata.

## Step 2 — Guided practice

Draw SP-initiated sign-in, then test a disposable IdP with valid and invalid audience assertions.

Use synthetic data, a disposable environment and authorized accounts only. Record expected behavior before execution.

## Step 3 — Acceptance

**Positive:** A valid assertion establishes the intended identity only.

**Negative:** Wrong issuer, expired or replayed assertion must be denied.

Inspect the complementary `enterpriseAccess` teaching model. Extend its assertions when relevant; it is not an implementation of the full external technology.

## Qualification boundary

Synthetic policy check on already-validated claims only; no actual SAML assertion, SCIM, MFA, token or identity-provider verification.

## Applied engineering walkthrough

SAML federates authentication between an identity provider and a service provider. A signed assertion is not automatically valid for every service or every request. Use a maintained library and trusted metadata to check issuer, signature, audience, recipient, time, correlation and replay as appropriate to the selected flow.

### Diagnose the concrete failure

Draw a synthetic SP-initiated flow and test a permitted disposable IdP configuration. Compare a valid response with wrong audience, expired assertion and repeated assertion. Confirm that rejected responses create no application session.

### Independent exercise and evidence

Write an IdP trust configuration and rotation checklist. Record sanitized assertion validation outcomes and replay handling; never log raw assertions or treat a decoded identity as an authorized organization member.

**Evidence boundary:** These are practice instructions, not recorded results. Preserve draft status and independently verify actual IdP, application authorization, database and reviewer evidence.
