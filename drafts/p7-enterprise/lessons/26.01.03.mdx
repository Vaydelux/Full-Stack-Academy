# Domain verification

> Instructor draft. Local models do not qualify external identity, billing, privacy compliance or an actual capstone release.

## Learning outcomes

Explain the engineering decision, reproduce or design the positive and negative experiment, and identify the remaining evidence.

## Context

An admin claims a company's email domain.

## Step 1 — Mental model

Domain ownership proof and organization membership are distinct; verify a scoped expiring challenge using the intended method.

## Step 2 — Guided practice

Design a synthetic DNS challenge with random token, expiration and tenant binding; test mismatched token.

Use synthetic data, a disposable environment and authorized accounts only. Record expected behavior before execution.

## Step 3 — Acceptance

**Positive:** Only the intended claimant completes verification.

**Negative:** Email suffix alone cannot grant admin rights.

Inspect the complementary `enterpriseAccess` teaching model. Extend its assertions when relevant; it is not an implementation of the full external technology.

## Qualification boundary

Synthetic policy check on already-validated claims only; no actual SAML assertion, SCIM, MFA, token or identity-provider verification.

## Knowledge check

1. What is the invariant and trust boundary?
2. Which negative result falsifies an unsafe design?
3. Which real tool or reviewer must supply missing evidence?

**Assessment:** Record exact fixture input, expected and actual output, negative evidence and not-run checks. Do not substitute a design or model for a real acceptance result.
