# Organization policy administration

> Instructor draft. Local models do not qualify external identity, billing, privacy compliance or an actual capstone release.

## Learning outcomes

Explain the engineering decision, reproduce or design the positive and negative experiment, and identify the remaining evidence.

## Context

A tenant admin edits organization login requirements.

## Step 1 — Mental model

Policy changes require authorization, scoped validation, safe rollout and recovery from lockout.

## Step 2 — Guided practice

Design versioned policy updates with actor and change reason; test foreign-tenant change and recovery account policy.

Use synthetic data, a disposable environment and authorized accounts only. Record expected behavior before execution.

## Step 3 — Acceptance

**Positive:** Authorized tenant change is recorded.

**Negative:** Cross-tenant update or irreversible lockout fails.

Inspect the complementary `enterpriseAccess` teaching model. Extend its assertions when relevant; it is not an implementation of the full external technology.

## Qualification boundary

Synthetic policy check on already-validated claims only; no actual SAML assertion, SCIM, MFA, token or identity-provider verification.

## Applied engineering walkthrough

Enterprise policy changes can affect every member of an organization and may accidentally lock out administrators. Require action-level authorization, validated scoped changes, explicit version checks and a controlled recovery path. A privileged session for one tenant does not confer authority over another.

### Diagnose the concrete failure

Prepare a versioned synthetic policy update and try a stale revision, unauthorized actor and foreign organization. Verify rejected updates leave the prior policy active and record a safe diagnostic.

### Independent exercise and evidence

Design staged enforcement, notification and a tightly controlled recovery mechanism. Record change reason, previous and new version, authorized approver and rollback constraints; test lockout recovery without weakening normal controls.

**Evidence boundary:** These are practice instructions, not recorded results. Preserve draft status and independently verify actual IdP, application authorization, database and reviewer evidence.
