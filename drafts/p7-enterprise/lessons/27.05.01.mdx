# Bug bounty style review

> Instructor draft. Local models do not qualify external identity, billing, privacy compliance or an actual capstone release.

## Learning outcomes

Explain the engineering decision, reproduce or design the positive and negative experiment, and identify the remaining evidence.

## Context

A reviewer searches for weaknesses in a learner project.

## Step 1 — Mental model

Authorized security review has defined scope, synthetic accounts, impact limits and responsible reporting.

## Step 2 — Guided practice

Review disposable app for auth, input, secrets and recovery defects; record reproducible findings and fixes.

Use synthetic data, a disposable environment and authorized accounts only. Record expected behavior before execution.

## Step 3 — Acceptance

**Positive:** Findings have impact, reproduction and regression tests.

**Negative:** Testing unapproved external systems or unverifiable claims fails.

## Qualification boundary

Final defense rubric only; actual human review, exploit verification, performance, costs and upgrade rehearsal remain separate.

## Applied engineering walkthrough

A security review is an authorized investigation of a defined candidate and its trust boundaries, not an unrestricted attempt to break unrelated systems. Start with synthetic identities, an isolated environment, explicit impact limits and a reporting path. Prioritize reproducible violations of authentication, authorization, input handling and secret management.

### Diagnose the concrete failure

Review the disposable workspace using two synthetic organizations, direct protected requests and malformed inputs. Document one reproduced defect or explicitly record that the selected test did not reproduce one. If a defect is found, add a failing regression before the correction and rerun it afterward.

### Independent exercise and evidence

Produce a scoped finding record with candidate revision, expected and observed behavior, reproduction, impact, fix and retest. Record unresolved risks and review ownership without including real credentials or private customer records.

**Evidence boundary:** These are practice instructions, not recorded results. Preserve draft status and independently verify actual implementation, review, recovery, cost and candidate evidence.

## Knowledge check

1. What is the invariant and trust boundary?
2. Which negative result falsifies an unsafe design?
3. Which real tool or reviewer must supply missing evidence?

**Assessment:** Record exact fixture input, expected and actual output, negative evidence and not-run checks. Do not substitute a design or model for a real acceptance result.
