# Architecture decision record

> Instructor draft. Local models do not qualify external identity, billing, privacy compliance or an actual capstone release.

## Learning outcomes

Explain the engineering decision, reproduce or design the positive and negative experiment, and identify the remaining evidence.

## Context

A team must choose a frontend/backend stack.

## Step 1 — Mental model

An ADR records problem, alternatives, decision, consequences and conditions for reversal.

## Step 2 — Guided practice

Compare a Next-only service with Next plus Nest backend; record constraints and why one is selected.

Use synthetic data, a disposable environment and authorized accounts only. Record expected behavior before execution.

## Step 3 — Acceptance

**Positive:** Choice is tied to scope and tradeoffs.

**Negative:** Framework preference without reasons or migration cost fails.

Inspect the complementary `webAcceptance` teaching model. Extend its assertions when relevant; it is not an implementation of the full external technology.

## Qualification boundary

## Applied engineering walkthrough

An architecture decision record makes a tradeoff explicit rather than awarding points for extra services. A single Next application can reduce operational overhead; a separate Nest API can establish a useful service boundary when ownership, independent clients or workload justify it. Compare alternatives using deployment, data ownership, testing and failure behavior.

### Diagnose the concrete failure

Sketch the same task creation journey in both designs, including validation, authorization and persistence. Identify the extra network failure in the separated design and the coupling risk in the single-process design.

### Independent exercise and evidence

Write an ADR with constraints, chosen option, consequences and measurable reversal criteria. Preserve stable business contracts so a later extraction is possible without speculative infrastructure; record unresolved operational evidence.

**Evidence boundary:** These are practice instructions, not recorded results. Preserve draft status and verify actual application, browser and human review evidence independently.

## Knowledge check

1. What is the invariant and trust boundary?
2. Which negative result falsifies an unsafe design?
3. Which real tool or reviewer must supply missing evidence?

**Assessment:** Record exact fixture input, expected and actual output, negative evidence and not-run checks. Do not substitute a design or model for a real acceptance result.
