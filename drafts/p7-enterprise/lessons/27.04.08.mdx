# Production readiness review

> Instructor draft. Local models do not qualify external identity, billing, privacy compliance or an actual capstone release.

## Learning outcomes

Explain the engineering decision, reproduce or design the positive and negative experiment, and identify the remaining evidence.

## Context

A learner reviews production readiness.

## Step 1 — Mental model

Acceptance includes build, security, observability, rollback and tested restore, plus manual review of remaining risks.

## Step 2 — Guided practice

Run productionAcceptance; attach actual candidate-specific evidence and signoff for each item.

Use synthetic data, a disposable environment and authorized accounts only. Record expected behavior before execution.

## Step 3 — Acceptance

**Positive:** All required real checks pass or remain explicitly blocked.

**Negative:** Missing restore, failed security or false evidence cannot authorize launch.

Inspect the complementary `productionAcceptance` teaching model. Extend its assertions when relevant; it is not an implementation of the full external technology.

## Qualification boundary

Capstone readiness rubric only; no container, deployment, scan, benchmark or restore is verified by supplied booleans.

## Applied engineering walkthrough

Production acceptance combines exact-candidate build evidence, security checks, observability, workload results, rollback and tested recovery with independent review. The productionAcceptance model evaluates supplied evidence flags; it does not run infrastructure or prove that the recorded evidence is genuine. Missing gates must remain visible.

### Diagnose the concrete failure

Run the rubric with a missing restore or security item and verify it cannot approve release. Gather actual results from the disposable stack, including one failed smoke and recovery case, and bind them to the candidate source and artifact.

### Independent exercise and evidence

Assemble a release packet with owner, commands, runtime versions, evidence references and blocking defects. Record an explicit go or no-go decision with reviewer identity; preserve the academy deployment freeze until the real publication gates pass.

**Evidence boundary:** These are practice instructions, not recorded results. Preserve draft status and independently verify actual infrastructure, build, security, performance, recovery and reviewer evidence.
