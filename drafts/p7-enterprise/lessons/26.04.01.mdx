# Audit search and exports

> Instructor draft. Local models do not qualify external identity, billing, privacy compliance or an actual capstone release.

## Learning outcomes

Explain the engineering decision, reproduce or design the positive and negative experiment, and identify the remaining evidence.

## Context

An auditor searches privileged activity.

## Step 1 — Mental model

Audit queries need tenant scope, bounded filters, pagination, redaction and export authorization.

## Step 2 — Guided practice

Design synthetic audit rows for two tenants and a scoped query/export contract.

Use synthetic data, a disposable environment and authorized accounts only. Record expected behavior before execution.

## Step 3 — Acceptance

**Positive:** Only permitted records and fields appear.

**Negative:** Foreign-tenant records or secrets in export fail.

Inspect the complementary `adminAllowed` teaching model. Extend its assertions when relevant; it is not an implementation of the full external technology.

## Qualification boundary

Synthetic tenant-admin authorization model only; actual RBAC, audit integrity, support workflows and migration safety require runtime acceptance.

## Applied engineering walkthrough

An audit console is itself a privileged data access surface. Scope every query and export to the authorized tenant, bound time ranges and page sizes, and use stable ordering with a unique tie breaker. Redact sensitive fields before serialization; a frontend filter cannot enforce tenant isolation.

### Diagnose the concrete failure

Seed two synthetic tenants with overlapping action types and timestamps. Query one tenant with a bounded filter, then supply the other tenant's identifier and an invalid cursor. Verify no foreign records appear in either JSON or export.

### Independent exercise and evidence

Define search, pagination and export permissions separately. Record expected result IDs, redacted fields and denied requests, including a role revoked between viewing the list and requesting an export.

**Evidence boundary:** These are practice instructions, not recorded results. Preserve draft status and independently verify actual API, database, authorization, browser and reviewer evidence.
