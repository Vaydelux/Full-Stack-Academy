# Right to export/delete

> Instructor draft. Local models do not qualify external identity, billing, privacy compliance or an actual capstone release.

## Learning outcomes

Explain the engineering decision, reproduce or design the positive and negative experiment, and identify the remaining evidence.

## Context

A person requests access to or deletion of account data.

## Step 1 — Mental model

Verify requester identity, scope, exemptions and legal holds; coordinate across systems and record disposition.

## Step 2 — Guided practice

Create a synthetic export request and deletion workflow with hold and backup handling.

Use synthetic data, a disposable environment and authorized accounts only. Record expected behavior before execution.

## Step 3 — Acceptance

**Positive:** Only authorized export is delivered; deletion eligibility reviewed.

**Negative:** Foreign-account export or ignoring a legal hold fails.

Inspect the complementary `retentionDecision` teaching model. Extend its assertions when relevant; it is not an implementation of the full external technology.

## Qualification boundary

Synthetic retention review model only; not legal advice or proof of compliance. Verify current law and organization-specific obligations with qualified counsel.

## Applied engineering walkthrough

A data access or deletion request needs verified identity, defined scope, review of applicable exceptions and an execution plan covering every relevant system. Deletion is not the same as hiding a record. Backups, legal holds and asynchronous processors require explicit disposition rather than unsupported promises of instant erasure.

### Diagnose the concrete failure

Create a synthetic request spanning an account database, object storage and backup. Include a held record and an unavailable processor; check that the workflow records each unresolved item without declaring completion prematurely.

### Independent exercise and evidence

Design an approval and evidence workflow with scoped exports, secure delivery, retry handling and a reviewed backup policy. Record disposition for each system and separate legal decisions from technical execution.

**Evidence boundary:** These are practice instructions, not recorded results. Preserve draft status; actual controls, legal applicability, runtime and independent review remain separate gates.
