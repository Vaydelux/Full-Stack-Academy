# Live updates

> Instructor draft. Local models do not qualify external identity, billing, privacy compliance or an actual capstone release.

## Learning outcomes

Explain the engineering decision, reproduce or design the positive and negative experiment, and identify the remaining evidence.

## Context

A teammate receives task changes in real time.

## Step 1 — Mental model

Live updates need authorization, scoped subscriptions, versioning and reconnect reconciliation.

## Step 2 — Guided practice

Use local websocket/SSE fixture with two tenants; drop connection and replay missed updates.

Use synthetic data, a disposable environment and authorized accounts only. Record expected behavior before execution.

## Step 3 — Acceptance

**Positive:** Authorized updates arrive and reconnect converges.

**Negative:** Foreign event or stale state after reconnect fails.

Inspect the complementary `workspaceAcceptance` teaching model. Extend its assertions when relevant; it is not an implementation of the full external technology.
