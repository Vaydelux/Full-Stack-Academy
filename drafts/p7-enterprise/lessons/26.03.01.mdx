# Data inventories

> Instructor draft. Local models do not qualify external identity, billing, privacy compliance or an actual capstone release.

## Learning outcomes

Explain the engineering decision, reproduce or design the positive and negative experiment, and identify the remaining evidence.

## Context

An organization cannot explain where personal data is stored.

## Step 1 — Mental model

A data inventory identifies data categories, purpose, systems, recipients, owners, retention and access paths.

## Step 2 — Guided practice

Map synthetic account, billing and log datasets; assign owner and legitimate purpose.

Use synthetic data, a disposable environment and authorized accounts only. Record expected behavior before execution.

## Step 3 — Acceptance

**Positive:** Each dataset has documented processing and access boundaries.

**Negative:** Unknown collection or unowned sensitive dataset fails review.

Inspect the complementary `retentionDecision` teaching model. Extend its assertions when relevant; it is not an implementation of the full external technology.

## Qualification boundary

Synthetic retention review model only; not legal advice or proof of compliance. Verify current law and organization-specific obligations with qualified counsel.

## Applied engineering walkthrough

A data inventory is a map of actual data flows, not just a list of database tables. Record purpose, category, collection source, storage, recipients, access roles, owner and retention for each dataset. Include operational logs, backups and third-party processing so an apparently small feature does not hide additional copies.

### Diagnose the concrete failure

Map synthetic account, billing and diagnostic datasets through collection, processing, support and backup. Identify one field with no documented purpose and one copy omitted from the initial diagram.

### Independent exercise and evidence

Create an inventory with accountable owners and review dates. Compare it against the actual schema and configuration of a disposable service, recording unknown processing destinations and unresolved review questions.

**Evidence boundary:** These are practice instructions, not recorded results. Preserve draft status; actual controls, legal applicability, runtime and independent review remain separate gates.
