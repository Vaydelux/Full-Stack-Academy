## The problem we're solving

A team repeatedly debates caching without documenting the original experiment. A short record of what was observed and why a decision was proposed prevents repeated guesswork.

## Build the mental model

An experiment log preserves the hypothesis, environment, procedure, measurements and limitations. An ADR records context, decision status, tradeoffs and reversal strategy. Proposed is not the same as accepted. One local synthetic benchmark does not prove performance under production traffic. Never include secret configuration values or customer data in committed experiment logs.

**Predict before running:** A fast warm-cache local benchmark is evidence only for that tested workload, not a guarantee that every production operation is faster.

## Bug Hunt: isolate and repair

A proposed ADR claims 'Redis is always faster' after one tiny local test. Document dataset, limitations and alternatives, and keep status proposed until the evidence is reviewed.
