## The problem we're solving

A status tile slides by changing left, forcing a layout-related geometry change for every motion frame. A code review should require a reproducible symptom and realistic verification, not only a polished image. The local fixture is synthetic, deterministic and offline.

## Build the mental model

Rendering has distinct style, layout, paint and compositing work. Animating transform often avoids repeated layout calculation compared with changing left or width, but a browser may still paint or choose different layers. Do not claim an animation is GPU accelerated without inspecting the actual browser trace.

**Predict before running:** The marker moves through transform without changing its layout position property; reduced motion disables interpolation.

## Explore and verify

Use DevTools to inspect computed styles and the Performance recording when making speed claims. Not every transform is composited; not every font loading strategy removes CLS; not every visually distinct control is accessible. The Node tests check instructor patterns; the isolated browser suite observes representative states. Manual assistive-technology and cross-browser checks remain outside the automated guarantee.
