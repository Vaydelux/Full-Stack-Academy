## The problem we're solving

An operation keeps resolving its result after the consumer cancels the request. This lab uses local Node.js timers, Promises and injected mock transport rather than relying on external APIs or a deployed site.

## Build the mental model

AbortController exposes a signal that communicating APIs may observe. Aborting does not automatically stop arbitrary code; your wrapper must listen, propagate the reason, reject promptly, and detach listeners on settlement. A pre-aborted signal must prevent work from starting.

**Predict before running:** A pre-cancelled operation never runs; an in-flight operation rejects when cancellation is signaled.
