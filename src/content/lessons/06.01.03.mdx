## The problem we're solving

A generic card drops secondary children because its body renders only one piece of content. This local fixture isolates a concrete failure before we introduce any external state or deployment.

## Build the mental model

The children prop allows parent components to provide arbitrary nested React nodes. Composition avoids an explosion of flags and keeps the wrapper independent of its content.

**Predict before running:** The repaired implementation respects its documented contract.
