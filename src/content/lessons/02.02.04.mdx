## The problem we're solving

A badge meant to sit in its card corner instead sticks to the viewport and overlaps navigation. The goal is a predictable local repair, not a decorative mockup. The HTML fixture is fully offline and has an intentionally failing starter plus an instructor solution.

## Build the mental model

An absolutely positioned element uses the containing block established by its positioned ancestor, whereas fixed positioning usually follows the viewport (subject to transformed ancestors). z-index participates in stacking contexts; a large numeric value cannot escape a lower ancestor stacking context. Keep the card positioned and its overlay local.

**Predict before running:** The badge is positioned inside the panel corner, not attached to the window.
