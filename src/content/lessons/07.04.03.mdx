## The problem we're solving

A generated sitemap includes lessons with placeholder content, even though these pages are marked noindex. This local fixture isolates a concrete failure before we introduce any external state or deployment.

## Build the mental model

Only canonical, published public URLs belong in a sitemap. Exclude planned or private pages and maintain consistent robots directives. A sitemap is a discovery hint; authorization is enforced by the server, not robots.txt.

**Predict before running:** The repaired implementation respects its documented contract.
