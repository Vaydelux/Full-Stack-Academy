## The problem we're solving

A data table with twelve columns overflows at 320px and hides critical record status beyond the horizontal viewport.

## Build the mental model

Prioritize essential columns and offer a card or definition-list alternative for narrow screens. Preserve relationships between labels and values; don't hide important row information just to eliminate horizontal scrolling.

**Predict first:** A narrow viewport selects labeled cards while a larger viewport may retain the data table.

<LessonStep number={2} title="Fix the policy and verify edge cases" file="labs/ui-mobile-table/starter/solution.ts" expected="A narrow viewport selects labeled cards while a larger viewport may retain the data table.">

```ts

export function solve(width:number):"table"|"cards"{if(!Number.isFinite(width)||width<0)throw new TypeError("Invalid width");return width<640?"cards":"table";}

```

**Why it works:** Prioritize essential columns and offer a card or definition-list alternative for narrow screens. Preserve relationships between labels and values; don't hide important row information just to eliminate horizontal scrolling.

**Proof:** Run `npm run lab:ui-mobile-table` and inspect the instructor test output.
